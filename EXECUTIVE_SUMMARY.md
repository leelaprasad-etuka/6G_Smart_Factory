Executive Summary: 6G Industrial Connectivity & Smart Manufacturing Policy

Briefing for Government, Telecommunications Regulators, and Industrial Infrastructure Stakeholders

1. Policy Purpose & Industrial Context
As national industrial strategies transition toward Industry 5.0 and 6G-enabled smart manufacturing, public and private infrastructure investments require empirical evidence on how wireless network quality impacts factory productivity. Based on 100,000 operational telemetry records from Thales Group smart manufacturing systems, this briefing outlines where 6G infrastructure investments yield the highest economic return.

2. Core Empirical Findings for Infrastructure Planning
High Resilience to Moderate Latency (1-50 ms): Modern edge-buffered industrial equipment maintains stable defect rates (~5.01%) and operational error rates (~7.50%) across latency variations from 1 ms to 50 ms. Public 6G testbeds do not need costly sub-millisecond optical backhaul for standard assembly workloads.

Packet Loss Reliability is More Critical Than Raw Latency: Packet loss exceeding 1.5% induces a measurable throughput loss of 2.11 units/hour per machine (dropping mean output from 277.37 to 275.26 units/hr). Scaled across thousands of industrial machines nationally, unmitigated packet loss (>1.5%) represents a significant cumulative GDP and manufacturing output drag.

Operational Error Suppression Drives High-Tier Efficiency: Only 2.99% of factory operating hours achieve High Efficiency (>=400 units/hr and <=2% error rate), while 77.83% remain in Low Efficiency primarily due to controller error rates averaging 8.93%. Pairing 6G connectivity grants with AI-driven machine error reduction is essential to unlock full productivity.

3. Policy & Regulatory Recommendations
Establish Industrial 6G Reliability Benchmarks (<1.5% Packet Loss Standard): Telecommunications regulators allocating industrial 6G spectrum should mandate packet loss thresholds below 1.5% for licensed smart factory slices rather than focusing solely on peak bandwidth or sub-5ms latency.

Targeted Subsidies for Dual-Objective Modernization: Industrial modernization grants should bundle 6G private network deployment with edge AI process-control upgrades. Connectivity alone cannot resolve mechanical/controller error rates (>5%) without integrated closed-loop automation.

Standardized Industrial Telemetry Auditing: Adopt the Network Stability Index (NSI) framework across public-private manufacturing corridors to prevent capital misallocation and distinguish telecommunications outages from plant-floor mechanical inefficiencies.
